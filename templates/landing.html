How It Works

1

Create Request

Fill out the request form with all required information and supporting documents.

2

Submit

Submit your request to the approval workflow. It automatically routes to the right approvers.

3

Review & Approve

Designated reviewers and approvers examine the request and approve or reject with feedback.

4

Complete

Approved requests are completed. Rejected requests can be edited and resubmitted.
